Drive times are derived from road data that contain information from OpenStreetMap, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors,
available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0): https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/ . Alaska and Hawaii
roads come from the Overture Maps Foundation transportation layer, which is derived from OpenStreetMap under the same
license. The drive-time tables are a Produced Work under the ODbL; this notice is the required attribution.

Other inputs are U.S. federal public data (U.S. Census Bureau, CMS, HRSA, SAMHSA FindTreatment.gov, USDA ERS) and the
author's own Project 1 release (CC BY 4.0, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24025395). No source data are endorsed by the agencies
that publish them.
